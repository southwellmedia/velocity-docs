## Moving a site to another template's look

Inside one template, new colours and fonts are a theme change. Another template's whole look is more than that:
it's also the template's stylesheet, layouts and sections. So you start from the other template and bring the
content across:

- **Blog posts, FAQs and pages** use Velocity's own collections in every template. Copy `src/content/blog/`,
  `src/content/faqs/` and `src/content/pages/`.
- **`src/config/site.config.ts`**: the name, URL, contact details and social links use the same fields everywhere.
- **Photos** in `src/assets/`.
- **Anything you built on Velocity or the Premium Kit**, since both are shared by every template. If the site is on
  an older Velocity, [upgrade](/cli/upgrade/) it first.

The business-specific content and config (menus, listings, journeys, products) and the template's own sections
are what make templates different, so those you redo.
