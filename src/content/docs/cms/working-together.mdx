Several people can have the same entry open, and an agent can change it over MCP while they do. Nobody is locked
out and nothing is saved over. The editor shows who is there, brings in what they save, and asks only when two
people changed the very same field.

There is nothing to switch on. It works on every site from 0.1.0-beta.24.

## Who is there

An open entry says who else has it open, and where in it they are:

- **At the top of the entry**: "Maya is here too", or "Claude is working on this" for an agent that just read or
  changed the entry over MCP.
- **On a field**: a mark with the person's name beside the field they are in.
- **On a section**: the same mark on the section's row in the form, in the list of sections of the visual editor,
  and as an outline on the page itself.

An agent that works in your own signed-in browser is shown as **Your agent**. An agent that uses an
[access token](/cms/agents/) or a connected app is shown by its name, at the top of the entry.

Presence is forgotten seconds after a tab is closed. It is kept apart from content: it has no history and never
reaches the site.

## What others save

What someone else saves appears in your open form within seconds, and in the page beside it, together with
whatever you have typed:

- a field one of you changed and the other did not,
- a field inside a section, however deep,
- a section added, moved or removed.

The editor says who changed what: "Maya changed Price", "Claude changed Sections", "You changed Title in another
tab". Undo still takes back only what you did.

While two people have one entry open, each one's typing is saved after about a second and reaches the other a
second or two later. It moves field by field, as saves: you do not see each other's letters or carets.

## When two people change the same field

Only then does the editor stop and ask. It shows the two versions side by side, says who saved the newer one, and
offers **Keep mine** or **Use the newer version**. Until you choose, nothing of yours is saved, by itself or by a
press of Save, and nothing you typed is lost.

Everything else in the entry is brought together without a question.

<Aside>
A tab left open for hours is safe. A form saves only after the version its values came from, so an older tab is
refused, takes in what was saved since, and saves again.
</Aside>

## Contacts, deals, forms and settings

The smaller windows follow the same idea in a simpler way. A window over a contact, a deal, a form's settings, the
pipeline's stages or a part of Settings holds only what you changed in it. Everything else follows what is saved:

- a change someone else makes meanwhile shows up in the open window,
- your save sends only what you changed, so theirs stays,
- when both of you changed the same thing, the window says so before you save.

These windows do not show who else has them open.

## Working beside an agent

Keep the editor open while you ask an agent to change the same page. What the agent saves arrives in your form
like anyone else's save, named as the agent's. If you were typing in a field the agent also changed, you are asked
which version stands.

An agent's changes are drafts until they are published, as yours are. See
[Working with an AI agent](/cms/agents/).

## What it does not do

- **No letter-by-letter typing.** Two people in one field see each other's work when it is saved, and are asked
  whose stands.
- **No locks.** Nobody can hold an entry or a field for themselves.
- **No presence outside entries.** Contacts, deals and settings are kept from overwriting each other, without the
  marks that say who is there.
